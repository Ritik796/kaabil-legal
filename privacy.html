Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

This explains what Ritik Parmar (sole proprietor) collects through the Kaabil app, why, and what you can do about it. It is written to be read, not to be got past.

What we collect from everybody

Your name, mobile number and email address, and a profile photo if you add one.

The addresses you save, including the map pin, so a pro can find the door.

Your bookings — what was asked for, when, what was agreed, what was paid, and the rating afterwards.

Messages you send through the app to the other side of a booking.

Basic technical information from the phone, such as the app version and whether notifications are allowed.

What we collect from pros

A government ID, front and back, and a photograph. The ID is stored so it can be checked; it is never shown to customers and the app cannot read it back.

Skills, experience, the area you work in and how far you will travel.

Payout details — a UPI id, or a bank account and IFSC — used only to pay you.

While you are on duty, your location, so the customer whose job you have taken can see you coming. It stops when you go off duty or the job ends.

Why we collect it

To match a booking to a pro and let the two of you reach each other.

To show where a pro is while they are on the way.

To keep a record of what was agreed and paid, and to send a receipt.

To keep the service safe — an ID on file means somebody can be identified if something goes wrong.

Who else sees it

The other side of your booking sees what they need to do the job: a pro sees your name, number and address; you see the pro's name, number, photo and rating. Neither of you sees anything about anyone else.

Our database, file storage and sign-in run on Supabase.

Signing in with Google uses Google Sign-In; signing in with Truecaller uses Truecaller, which tells us the number it has verified and the name on that account.

Maps and addresses use Google Maps.

When online payments are switched on, the payment itself is handled by the payment provider — card and bank details never reach us.

We do not sell your data, and we do not hand it to advertisers.

Your location

A customer's location is used when you pick an address on the map, and only then.

A pro's location is sent while they are on duty, and a notification shows on the phone the whole time it is happening — so it is never doing it quietly.

You can turn the permission off in Android settings. The app still works; a pro simply cannot be tracked on the way, and that is the part that stops.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept while your account exists, because they are what a receipt or a complaint is checked against.

A pro's ID is kept while they are active on the app and deleted when their account is closed.

Ask us to delete your account and we delete your profile, addresses and messages, and anonymise the bookings we have to keep for our own records.

What you can ask for

A copy of what we hold about you.

A correction — most of it you can change yourself in the app.

Deletion of your account and the data that goes with it.

Write to ritikparmar7073@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

Data is held on Supabase, and the database only lets an account read its own rows — signed in or not, nobody can pull somebody else's profile out of the app.

IDs sit in storage that the app can write to and cannot read from.

No system is perfect, and we will not claim ours is. If something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Children

Kaabil is not for anybody under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and we will say so in the app when the change matters.

Questions, or anything above: ritikparmar7073@gmail.com
